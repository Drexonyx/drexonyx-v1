## 2. PLATFORM STRATEGIES

### Instagram

**Goal:** Build brand awareness and a following before launch; generate inquiries via DM and link in bio

**Content types:**
- Render reveals (CGI walkthrough stills → use as carousel)
- Construction progress (timelapse or weekly update reels)
- Location context (what's nearby: schools, metro, malls)
- Interior design close-ups (kitchen, bathroom, balcony)
- "Day in the life" lifestyle concept reels (30–60 seconds)

**Frequency:** 4–5 posts/week + 3 Stories/day
**Format priority:** Reels (1080×1920) > Carousels > Static posts
**Hashtag strategy:** Mix of high-volume (#DubaiRealEstate, #DubaiProperty) + niche (#JVCDubai, #DubaiApartments) + brand hashtag

### Facebook

**Goal:** Lead generation — capture name, email, WhatsApp number of potential buyers

**Content types:**
- Same creative as Instagram (cross-post with captions adjusted)
- Lead Generation Ads (Meta Ads Manager, not organic) — form fills
- Video ads (render walkthrough + voiceover, 60–90 seconds)
- Arabic-language ad variants for local audience

**Paid setup (recommended, client must approve budget):**
- Campaign objective: Lead Generation
- Audience targeting: UAE residents, age 28–55, household income indicators, interests: property investment, Dubai lifestyle
- Lookalike audience: build from existing client/lead database once available
- Ad format: Video + instant form or WhatsApp click-to-chat

**No boost/paid ads in this strategy plan — client must approve budget separately.**

### YouTube

**Goal:** Trust-building through long-form content; supports paid retargeting

**Content types:**
- Developer brand film (2–3 minutes): who we are, why this development, why Dubai now
- Community tour (3–5 minutes): walkthrough of the neighborhood, nearby amenities
- Q&A / FAQ (2–3 minutes): answering "Is Dubai real estate a good investment in 2026?"
- Construction update series (1–2 minutes monthly): builds anticipation and trust

**Frequency:** 1–2 videos per month
**YouTube Shorts:** Repurpose Instagram Reels to YouTube Shorts (no extra production)
