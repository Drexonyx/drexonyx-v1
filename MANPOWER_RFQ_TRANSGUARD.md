## Additional Requirement — Due Diligence (Mandatory for This Inquiry)

Before this RFQ can proceed to evaluation, Transguard Group is required to provide written responses to the following additional due diligence questions. This requirement arises from public-domain reports documented by Migrant-Rights.org (2020–2021) and Equidem Research alleging:

- Nepali construction workers charged approximately NPR 78,000 in recruitment fees (the legal ceiling is NPR 10,000)
- Passport withholding
- Unpaid wages during COVID-related standdowns
- Substandard accommodation conditions

We note these allegations are from 2020–2021 and that no post-2021 independent corrective verification has been identified in available public sources.

### Due Diligence Questions (Mandatory)

**DD01.** Has Transguard Group conducted an internal investigation into the 2020–2021 Migrant-Rights.org / Equidem allegations? What were the findings and corrective actions taken?

**DD02.** Does Transguard Group maintain a published ILO/UNGP (UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights) compliance policy? Please provide or reference it.

**DD03.** Please provide a list of your current overseas recruitment agencies — specifically for India, Nepal, and Bangladesh — and confirm whether these agencies are independently audited for compliance with zero-fee-to-worker policy.

**DD04.** Can you provide an independent audit report or third-party certification (e.g., Social Responsibility Alliance, ELEVATE, Sedex) confirming zero worker-fee policy within the last 24 months?

**DD05.** Please confirm your current policy on passport retention. Are worker passports held by Transguard, by the worksite employer, or returned to the worker? What is the policy for early contract termination?

**Responses to DD01–DD05 must be provided before this inquiry can proceed to commercial evaluation.** Incomplete responses will result in suspension of this supplier from the selection process.
