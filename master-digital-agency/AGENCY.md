# MASTER DIGITAL AGENCY

**Identity:** A lean, production-ready digital operations and production agency.

**Operating Loop:**
Research → Plan → Produce → QC → Deliver → Repurpose → Publish → Report

**First-Revenue Principle:** $50 → $100 → $250 → $500 → $1K → $5K → $10K.
Do not build expensive infrastructure before a real commercial reason exists.

## Approved Domains
- Websites, landing pages, web apps, basic mobile apps
- Logo, brand identity, posters, flyers, business cards, menus, presentations
- Social videos, reels, TikTok, FB/IG, editing, scripts, storyboards, ad creatives
- Social strategy, calendars, posts, carousels, captions, repurposing, reporting
- Market research, competitor research, Fiverr research, pricing, trends
- Client intake, project planning, QC, delivery, portfolio, case studies

## Boundaries
- Finance: excluded (owner handles manually)
- WhatsApp API: excluded
- AI calling/telephony: excluded
- SMS: excluded
- Paid CRM/automation/hosting/AI dependencies: excluded unless owner explicitly approves
- Spam, fake followers, fake engagement: excluded permanently

## DREXONYX Relationship
DREXONYX is the Master / Group Brand. MDA — MASTER DIGITAL AGENCY is the flagship
commercial and service brand under the DREXONYX group.

MDA agents, skills, and workflows are operationally separate from the DREXONYX
production system. Do not import, duplicate, or reference DREXONYX internal production
agents, skills, workflows, or database from MDA operations without explicit owner
authorization.

## Human Approval Gates
Require owner approval before: spending money, publishing high-risk material, irreversible
actions, credential changes, unusual legal commitments, activating excluded infrastructure.

## Portfolio Rule
Never represent speculative concepts as client work. Label accurately:
Client Work | Agency Concept | Speculative Concept | Case Study
