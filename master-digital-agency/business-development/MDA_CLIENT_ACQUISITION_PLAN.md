## 4. Target Customers

Primary targets are small and medium-sized businesses in Dubai and the UAE, particularly:

- **Newly established companies** — need a first professional presence.
- **Cleaning and maintenance companies** — active market in UAE, often under-branded.
- **Business and professional services** — consultants, HR, accounting, legal support.
- **Consulting companies** — frequently need credential documentation.
- **Any business with an outdated, incomplete, or visually poor existing profile.**

Secondary targets (later phase):
- Restaurants opening new locations.
- Healthcare clinics and specialist centers.
- Real estate and property management.

## 5. Sales Approach

### 5a. Principles
- No paid advertisements initially.
- Contact prospects individually with relevant, personalized outreach.
- Show relevant samples (MDA catalog, NEXORA pitch deck as design proof).
- Issue a clear written quotation and scope before starting.
- Collect an advance payment (minimum 50%) before work begins.
- Record every outreach, reply, quotation, objection, and paid conversion.

### 5b. Channels to investigate (prioritized)
1. **Direct outreach via LinkedIn** — identify business owners directly, message with context.
2. **Facebook groups** — UAE business owner groups where promotion is permitted.
3. **WhatsApp outreach** — only to publicly listed business numbers found on websites/Google Maps.
4. **Google Maps prospecting** — find businesses in Dubai with missing or low-quality profiles.
5. **Local business directories** — Yello.ae, Pages.ae, Dubai Chamber directory.

### 5c. What NOT to do
- Do not message anyone through client portals, CRMs, or private data sources.
- Do not send bulk or unsolicited mass messages.
- Do not misrepresent MDA as a large agency — present accurately as a professional design studio.
- Do not begin work without confirming payment terms in writing.

## 8. Client Intake Checklist

Before starting any project, confirm the following from the client:

**Company information**
- [ ] Full legal company name
- [ ] Industry / business type
- [ ] Services or products offered
- [ ] Founding year (optional)
- [ ] Company tagline or slogan (if any)

**Brand assets**
- [ ] Company logo (high-resolution, vector preferred)
- [ ] Brand colors (hex codes or Pantone, if known)
- [ ] Existing brand guidelines (if any)
- [ ] Previous profile or brochure (if any — for reference)

**Content**
- [ ] Company overview / about text
- [ ] Services list with brief descriptions
- [ ] Team photos (optional)
- [ ] Client logos or testimonials (optional)
- [ ] Contact details (phone, email, website, address, social media)

**Preferences**
- [ ] Language(s): English only / Arabic only / Bilingual
- [ ] Any competitor profiles they like (for style reference)
- [ ] Any styles or colors to avoid

**Commercial**
- [ ] Advance payment received (50%)
- [ ] Written confirmation of scope agreed
