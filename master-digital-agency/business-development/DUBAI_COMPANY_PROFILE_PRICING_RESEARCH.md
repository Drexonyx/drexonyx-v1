## Research Limitations — Read First

**Network access:** Direct website fetches failed for all `.ae` domains and most non-US domains from the research environment. All pricing data below is sourced from Google search snippets, structured data, and page previews — not from directly reading each provider's pricing page. This means:
- Figures may not reflect the most current published prices.
- Exact package inclusions and fine print could not be verified on the page.
- Pages without markup or meta descriptions may have been missed entirely.

**What was searched:** Six separate web searches ran against Google using terms including `company profile design price Dubai AED`, `UAE company profile design packages pages revisions`, and targeted site queries. Results were cross-checked across multiple search passes.

**What cannot be concluded:** This sample does not represent the full Dubai market. Only providers that appeared in Google results were captured. Providers that rely on word of mouth, WhatsApp-only inquiries, or unindexed pages are not included. Prices published on vendor sites are marketing prices and may not reflect actual negotiated rates.

## Summary of Findings

**Lowest verified published price (design only):** AED 800 (Integrate IT Solutions, 8 pages)
**Lowest published bundle price (design + content):** AED 400 (companyprofilemaker.ae, 4–5 pages promotional rate — treat with caution)
**Highest price in this sample:** AED 6,000 (Digital Shack, 12-page complex with illustrations)

**Common price bands:**
- Design only, up to 8 pages: **AED 800–1,599**
- Design only, 10–16 pages: **AED 1,500–2,800**
- Design + content, mid-range: **AED 1,800–2,800**
- Premium / complex: **AED 2,500–6,000**

**Typical included deliverables:**
- Print-ready PDF (all providers)
- 2 revision rounds (implied; often not explicitly stated)
- Source files: rarely included in base price

**What cannot be concluded:**
- Whether these prices include 5% UAE VAT.
- Current vs outdated status of any specific price.
- The full range of Dubai providers (only those indexed by Google are captured).
- Whether any provider's stated price is the actual transacted price after negotiation.
- Whether design-only prices from non-content providers include layout/typography only or full visual design.

## Pricing Assessment for AED 650 Proposed Test Offer

**Scope for comparison:** 8 pages, design only, 2 revisions, PDF delivery.

| Benchmark | AED | vs AED 650 |
|---|---|---|
| Integrate IT Solutions (8 pages) | 800 | 19% cheaper |
| companyprofilemaker.ae (4–5 pages) | 899 | 28% cheaper |
| Dandelion starting price | 1,500 | 57% cheaper |
| Digital Shack (design only) | 2,000–3,500 | 67–81% cheaper |
| Fiverr UAE seller (4 pages) | ~440 | AED 650 is **higher** |

**Conclusion:** AED 650 for 8 pages is below the market minimum for design-only work from Dubai-based providers (AED 800 for comparable scope). It is above the Fiverr marketplace baseline (~AED 440 for fewer pages).

AED 650 is positioned as an intentional introductory price to attract a first client and build a UAE-based portfolio. It is not a sustainable long-term price for 8 pages of professional design work. Once 2–3 clients have been served, the price should be reviewed against the AED 800–1,599 market band for design-only work.
