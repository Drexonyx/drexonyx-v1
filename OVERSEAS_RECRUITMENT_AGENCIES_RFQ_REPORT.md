## WORKER RECRUITMENT FEE RISK — SYSTEMIC WARNING

Worker recruitment fees are a systemic, documented problem in the India/Bangladesh/Nepal/
Sri Lanka sourcing corridors. These fees, paid by workers to home-country sub-agents,
typically range from USD 1,000-4,000+ per worker.

**Legal position:**
- **UAE:** Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 Concerning the Regulation of Labour Relations,
  Article 6: Employers and recruitment agencies are prohibited from charging workers any fees.
- **ILO:** Dhaka Principles (2012) and ILO Recruitment Principles (2016) both prohibit worker
  fee charging.

**Practical situation:**
- NO agency in this research database confirmed a zero-fee policy in publicly available materials.
- Fees are typically paid by workers to home-country intermediaries (sub-agents), not directly
  to the UAE agency. UAE agencies may deny knowledge.
- This creates legal, reputational, and welfare risks for UAE employers.

**Required action:**
Before contracting any agency, obtain a written declaration confirming:
1. The agency does not charge workers any recruitment fees.
2. Home-country partners / sub-agents do not charge workers any fees.
3. The agency will provide evidence of payment flows if requested.

## MOHRE LICENCE VERIFICATION

No agency's MOHRE overseas recruitment licence was publicly confirmed via web research.
This is an important verification step before any contract is signed.

**How to verify:** Visit mohre.gov.ae → "Enquiries" → "Recruitment Agent Enquiry"
Search by company name or trade licence number.

A MOHRE-licensed overseas recruitment agency is legally permitted to recruit workers
from abroad on behalf of UAE employers. An unlicensed agency conducting overseas
recruitment is operating illegally.
