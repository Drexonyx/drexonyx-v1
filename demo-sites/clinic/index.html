About Us

Healthcare Held to the Highest Standard

Vitalis Medical Centre opened in 2012 with a founding belief: that patients in Dubai deserve the same standard of specialist care available in London, Mayo Clinic, or Singapore — without the flight.

Our consultants trained at the world's most respected institutions and bring that global perspective to every consultation. We invest continuously in diagnostic technology, and our facilities exceed Joint Commission International standards.

What sets us apart is not equipment — it's time. Our consultants are never double-booked. Every patient receives a full thirty-minute consultation, minimum.
