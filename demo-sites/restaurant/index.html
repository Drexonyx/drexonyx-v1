The Story Behind the Dish

Fourteen Hours of Patience

Our signature Ouzi Royale is prepared using a recipe passed down through three generations of our founding family from the Levant — a whole young lamb, slow-roasted overnight in a sealed underground pit over fragrant hardwood and desert herbs.

"Food is not fuel — it is memory, ceremony, and love made edible. We serve all three, every evening."
Chef Mahmoud Al-Rashid
Executive Chef & Co-Founder
