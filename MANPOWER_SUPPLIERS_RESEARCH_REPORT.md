## Regulatory Notes

- **UAE MOHRE Temporary Staffing licence** requires AED 1,000,000 bank guarantee.
- **Overseas hiring** uses 2-year work permit; worker fees are explicitly prohibited under UAE law.
- **Worker Fee Warning:** UAE law prohibits charging workers any placement or recruitment fee.
  Risk is highest with India/Nepal/Bangladesh sourcing pipelines. Always require a zero-fee
  policy in writing covering all in-country agents.
- **MOHRE Call Centre:** 600590000
