Critical Conditions
No Conventional Per-Worker Placement Fee (Q7)

Standard UAE overseas recruitment agencies charge AED 1,500–5,000 per worker. This is rejected. RFQ Q7 asks for an alternative B2B commercial structure (project/batch fee, service retainer, cost-recovery model). Q8 asks what the agency proposes.

Worker Fees Illegal Under UAE Law (Q9)

Federal Decree-Law 33/2021, Art. 6. Systemic in India/Nepal/Bangladesh corridors. Written zero-fee declaration required from agency AND all home-country partners. Non-negotiable.

MOHRE Licence Required

No agency's UAE overseas recruitment licence publicly confirmed. Verify at mohre.gov.ae before contracting. Agencies without MOHRE licence cannot legally conduct overseas recruitment for UAE employers.
