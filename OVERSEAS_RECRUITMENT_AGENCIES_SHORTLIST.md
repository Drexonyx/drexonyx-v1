## CRITICAL COMPLIANCE NOTES FOR ALL AGENCIES

1. **Worker recruitment fees are illegal under UAE law** (Federal Decree-Law 33/2021, Article 6). No agency confirmed a zero-fee policy in research. Require a written declaration.
2. **MOHRE overseas recruitment licence**: No agency's licence was publicly confirmed. Verify directly via MOHRE portal (mohre.gov.ae) before signing.
3. **Home-country regulations**: Nepal (DOFE), India (MOIA/MEA RPOA), Bangladesh (BAIRA/BMET), Philippines (POEA) all require agency and/or employer registration. Do not source from these corridors without verifying both agency and employer registration.
