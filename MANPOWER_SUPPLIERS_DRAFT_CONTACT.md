## Draft Message — Body

```
Dear [Company Name] Team,

I am reaching out on behalf of a client company to enquire about your capacity to supply
manpower for the following requirement:

POSITION DETAILS:
- Role: Construction Site Helpers (Male)
- Quantity: 100 workers
- Contract: 2 years
- Site Type: Oil & Gas construction site
- Location: UAE (exact location shared after NDA/LOI)

COMPENSATION & BENEFITS (provided by our client):
- Basic salary: AED 1,000/month
- Working hours: 8 hours/day + overtime (~2 hours daily), 6 days/week
- Employment visa: Provided
- Accommodation: Provided at site
- Local transport: Provided
- Medical insurance: Provided
- Workers' compensation: Covered
- Food: Free at site
- Probation: 6 months | Annual leave: 30 days paid (after 2-year completion)
- Monthly performance bonus: AED 50 (subject to stated conditions)

IMPORTANT — WORKER FEE POLICY:
- Zero recruitment/placement fees charged to workers — in UAE and in source countries.
- Please confirm this in writing, including your in-country agent pipeline.
- UAE law prohibits any fees charged to workers (per MOHRE regulations).

INFORMATION REQUESTED:
1. Can you supply 100 construction site helpers for the above specifications?
2. What is your deployment timeline from agreement to mobilisation?
3. What source countries do you recruit from?
4. Can you share your MOHRE licence number and DED/trade licence?
5. What is your agency/service fee structure (charged to the employer, not workers)?

Please respond to this email or contact me at [CONTACT TO BE ADDED].

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[NAME TO BE ADDED]
[Drexonyx Studio — acting as intermediary on behalf of client]
```

---

## Notes on Draft

- Client identity is NOT disclosed in the initial message.
- MOHRE licence and DED verification request is built into the first contact.
- Worker fee policy confirmation is required in writing before any engagement.
- Source countries and deployment timeline are key qualification criteria.
- Do not mention the oil & gas site name or client company name at this stage.
