Delete Your DREXONYX Account

You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your projects, briefs, and order history will be permanently removed.

Option 1: Delete from the App (Fastest)

  1. Open the DREXONYX app
  2. Tap Account in the bottom navigation
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the menu
  4. Type DELETE to confirm
  5. Tap the red Permanently Delete Account button

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by email. Include the email address associated with your account.

Email Deletion Request

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by email.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, password hash)
  • All project briefs and uploaded files
  • Your project history and order records
  • All communication preferences

What We Retain

Under UAE commercial law, financial transaction records (invoice amounts, transaction IDs — not card details) must be retained for 7 years. These records are retained in anonymised form and cannot be used to identify you.
