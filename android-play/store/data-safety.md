## Data Sharing
We do not sell user data to third parties.

---

## Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
**Yes** — all data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.

---

## Does your app provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
**Yes** — from within the app (Account → Delete Account) and via https://www.drexonyx.com/account-deletion

---

## Is data collection required to use the app?
- **Account required for:** Project submission, dashboard, order history, payments
- **Account NOT required for:** Browsing home, services, portfolio, pricing, about, contact

---

## Are children a primary audience for your app?
**No** — the app is for business clients and professional service customers.

---

## Notes for Play review team
- No Google Play Billing — DREXONYX sells real-world professional creative services (video, photography, 3D) delivered outside the app. Per Google Play policy, real-world goods and services do not require Google Play in-app billing.
- Payments redirected to external browser (Ziina payment processor, UAE licensed).
- No gambling, no adult content, no user-to-user communication.
- Login is optional for browsing.
