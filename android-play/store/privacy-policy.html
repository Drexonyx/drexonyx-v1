Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

Drexonyx Studio FZ-LLC ("DREXONYX", "we", "our", "us") operates the DREXONYX mobile application ("App") and the website at www.drexonyx.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and a hashed password. We never store plain-text passwords.

Project briefs: Files, images, and text you upload when submitting a production brief.

Payment information: We do not store card details. Payments are processed by Ziina (a licensed UAE payment processor). We retain transaction IDs and invoice records only.

Device information: Device model, Android version, and network state — used for debugging and service optimisation. This data is not linked to advertising profiles.

Usage data: Which screens you visit within the App, for product improvement. No cross-app tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To deliver production services you have ordered
  • To process payments and send invoices
  • To send project status updates and service communications
  • To comply with legal obligations (UAE law, GDPR for EU residents)
  • To improve our App and services

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Ziina: Payment processing (UAE licensed)
  • Neon (database): Encrypted cloud database storage
  • Vercel: App hosting infrastructure
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. Data Retention

Account and project data is retained for as long as your account is active and for up to 7 years for financial records (UAE commercial law requirement). You may request deletion at any time.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export a copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, email privacy@drexonyx.com or use the account deletion flow in the App.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the App (Account → Delete Account), or by visiting www.drexonyx.com/account-deletion. Deletion is permanent and irreversible. Financial records required by UAE law are retained for 7 years in anonymised form.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, bcrypt hashing for passwords (cost factor 12), JWT tokens for authentication, and tenant isolation (you can never access another customer's data).

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International Transfers

Our servers are located in the United States (Vercel/AWS). By using the App you consent to this transfer. We apply appropriate safeguards consistent with GDPR requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of the App after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact
